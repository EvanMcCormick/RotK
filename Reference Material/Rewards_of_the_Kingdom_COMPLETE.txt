OBJECT OF THE GAME
To realistically show what it's like to be a Christian. Players receive the Gospel as they start down the GOSPEL ROAD. The game moves the players through the challenges of a daily walk with Christ along the PATH OF LIFE, and enables them to accumulate Stars for their Crowns.

SETTING UP THE GAME:
01. Shuffle ACTIVITY CARDS making sure the RAPTURE CARD is among them. Place these cards face down on the box marked ACTIVITY at the side of the game.
02. Shuffle the FRUIT CARDS and place them face down on the box marked FRUIT at the side of the game.
03. Shuffle the SCRIPTURE CARDS and place them face down on the box marked SCRIPTURE at the side of the Game.
04. Select a person to be the STEWARD, for the duration of the game, to handle the STAR POINT, CROWN CARDS, and other related duties.

WINNING THE GAME:
01. When the RAPTURE CARD appears, the player with the most Stars wins the Game.
02. To determine the winner, all players will add up all of their STAR POINTS for the STAR and CROWN CARDS they have collected. Any player holding SCRIPTURE OR FRUIT CARDS at the end of the Game will receive an additional 5 BONUS POINTS for each card they hold.
03. Any player who has accumulated all the FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT CARDS will receive an additional 150 STAR BONUS POINTS, that will be added to their final score.

STEWARD: GAME DUTIES:
01. The STEWARD will organize the STAR and CROWN CARDS into various piles so they can be handed out quickly, to all players.
02. The STEWARD will award CROWN CARDS to players as they pass over the CROWN spaces on the board.
03. The STEWARD will award the STAR POINTS from the ACTIVITY CARDS to the various players as they are won. To collect their STAR POINTS, all players will show the STEWARD the amount in the centre of the star, in the upper right hand corner of the ACTIVITY CARD.
04. The STEWARD is the OFFICIAL REFEREE OF THE GAME and has the power of FINAL DECISION in the event of a question.
05. When the first player enters the Heavenly City, the STEWARD will mark the length of time the Game will continue by the role of the die. An odd role will let the Game last for another 5 minutes, an even role will let the Game continue for another 10 minutes. The STEWARD will inform the players when time is up, and the Game is to end. (See notes: ENDING THE GAME)
06. The STEWARD will help the players tally their STAR POINTS at the end of the Game, to determine the WINNER.

PLAYING THE GAME:
01. Each player roles a die. The player who roles the highest number begins, with others taking their turn in a clockwise rotation.
02. Place the tokens on the start position indicated by the space called GOSPEL ROAD. All players go along the GOSPEL ROAD before receiving their first CROWN: the CROWN OF RIGHTEOUSNESS. No STARS can be accumulated until a player has this CROWN.
03. When the first CROWN is received the player starts down the PATH OF LIFE, and begins collecting the STARS necessary to win the Game. The player can also start collecting other CROWNS, FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT, SCRIPTURE and ACTIVITY CARDS if they are to be kept. (Example: Instant Means Of Escape Card)

STARS:
01. Stars are the REWARDS given for faithful service. When the RAPTURE CARD appears, the player with the most STAR POINTS, wins the Game.
02. The STAR POINT CARDS are controlled by the STEWARD. Players landing on a STAR VALUE CORNER, will receive their STAR POINTS from the STEWARD.
03. ACTIVITY CARDS which have point value will be collected from the STEWARD, simply by showing (him or her) the amount in the center of the STAR, on the upper right hand corner, of the ACTIVITY CARD.
04. Throughout the Game STAR CARDS may be traded in for higher STAR POINT VALUE CARDS. This will enable the winner to be determined more quickly, at the end of the game.
05. In the event of a tie, the die will decide the winner. Player with highest number rolled, wins the Game.
06. STAR POINTS once obtained can never be lost they will always be to your credit.
07. The STAR POINTS controlled by the STEWARD are 5, 10, 25, and 50. Any combination of these cards will make up the POINTS to be awarded to various players, throughout the game.

CROWN CARDS:
01. The CROWNS CARDS are received at various times throughout the Game and symbolize the REWARDS earned in our CHRISTIAN WALK. The first CROWN, the CROWN OF RIGHTEOUSNESS is received at the entrance to the PATH OF LIFE. The four remaining CROWNS are picked up as the players move around the board. In order to receive a CROWN the player needs only to pass over the CROWN SPACE to receive it. A direct landing on the space is not required.
02. If the player or the STEWARD have forgotten or missed a CROWN SPACE, the player is not penalized, and will be able to collect it from the STEWARD at any time.



10. WHAT ELSE HAS BEEN DONE TO SAVE TIME & MONEY?

The game REWARDS OF THE KINGDOM is copyrighted and all of the Game Rules, Booklet "Gospel Of The Game", card contents, and statistics from trial games played, are on I.B.M. computer.
